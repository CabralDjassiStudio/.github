# Cabral Djassi Studio Brand Policy

This policy applies to names, product names, logos, icons, mascots,
illustrations, videos, visual systems, and other brand identifiers.

## No automatic brand license

Access to a public repository, website, SDK, template, or open-source component
does not grant permission to use Cabral Djassi Studio brand assets.

Software rights and brand rights are separate.

## Permitted references

Accurate, non-misleading textual references may be used when necessary to:

- identify compatibility or integration;
- provide required attribution;
- link to an official repository or website;
- describe factual use of a product.

References must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, partnership,
employment, or official status.

## Written permission required

Permission is required to:

- use a Studio logo, icon, mascot, or visual identity;
- create a confusingly similar company, product, package, domain, or account name;
- produce merchandise or promotional materials;
- alter, recolor, animate, combine, or redistribute official visual assets;
- present a fork or derivative as an official Studio product;
- use the brand in advertising or commercial campaigns.

## Forks and integrations

A permitted fork or integration must use a distinct name and visual identity
unless written authorization states otherwise.

A disclaimer may be required:

> This project is independent and is not endorsed, sponsored, or maintained by
> Cabral Djassi Studio.

## Requests

Submit brand-use requests through an official channel available on the
[Cabral Djassi Studio website](https://cabraldjassi.com/).
