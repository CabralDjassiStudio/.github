# Cabral Djassi Studio Community Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Cabral Djassi Studio aims to maintain professional, inclusive, constructive, and
safe collaboration spaces. This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews, events, and other official community spaces.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate with respect, clarity, and good faith;
- critique work without attacking people;
- welcome different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and expertise levels;
- provide actionable feedback;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and security;
- avoid spam, intimidation, manipulation, and disruption;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- follow repository-specific licensing and contribution rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- impersonation, deception, or deliberate misrepresentation;
- trolling or coordinated disruption;
- exposing credentials, vulnerabilities, private code, or confidential data;
- retaliation against good-faith reporters;
- conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Reporting

Do not report sensitive conduct or security concerns through public issues.

Use an official private contact channel available on the
[Studio website](https://cabral-djassi-studio.vercel.app/).

Security vulnerabilities must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions,
issue warnings, restrict participation, ban accounts, or escalate serious
matters to the relevant platform or authority.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
