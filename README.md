## About

Cabral Djassi Studio is an independent digital product and engineering studio based in Cabo Verde.

We turn ideas and real-world challenges into thoughtful digital experiences, scalable software and dependable technology. Our work brings together product strategy, modern design and strong engineering to create solutions that are useful, accessible and built to evolve.

The name **Djassi** reflects identity, creativity and the ambition to build technology from our own perspective—rooted in Cabo Verde, connected to Africa and prepared for a global audience.

## What defines the Studio

- Purposeful product design
- Modern and accessible user experiences
- Reliable, secure and maintainable engineering
- Thoughtful use of artificial intelligence
- Scalable system architecture
- Interactive and motion-driven digital experiences
- Technology adapted to real people and real contexts

## Our approach

We look beyond the interface. Every product is considered as a complete system—from the experience people see to the architecture, security, performance and integrations supporting it.

We believe good technology should feel intuitive, work reliably and remain capable of evolving as its users and context change.

## Vision

Our vision is to demonstrate that ambitious, globally relevant technology can be imagined and built from Cabo Verde.

> Local identity. Thoughtful design. Global engineering.
